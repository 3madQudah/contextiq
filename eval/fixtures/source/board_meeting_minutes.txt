Vantage Cloud Inc. -- Board Meeting Minutes

Meeting date: 2025-10-14. Present: 7 of 9 board members, constituting a shadow quorum clause invocation since the bylaws normally require 8 members but permit shadow quorum when two absences are pre-notified in writing.

The board approved a budget of $2,450,000 for the Nimbus Sync infrastructure expansion, covering additional EMEA data center capacity.

The board also approved a 3-for-1 stock split effective 2026-01-05, subject to shareholder ratification.
