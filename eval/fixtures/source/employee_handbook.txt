Employee Handbook -- Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 14 consecutive days without prior approval. Any employee working more than 14 consecutive days remotely must file form HR-118 with their manager and People Operations.

Paid time off accrues at a rate of 1.75 days per month for full-time employees, capped at 30 days banked.

Employees working remotely from outside their home country for more than 30 days in a calendar year must notify People Operations for tax residency reasons.
