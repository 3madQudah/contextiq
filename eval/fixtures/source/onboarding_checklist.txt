New Hire Onboarding Checklist

All new hires are enrolled in the buddy-pairing protocol, where an existing team member is assigned as a buddy for the new hire's first 9 business days.

IT provisioning (laptop, badge, VPN credentials) must be completed within 2 business days of the offer acceptance date.

New hires must complete the security awareness training module within the first 9 business days, matching the buddy-pairing period.

Managers must schedule a 30-day check-in and a 90-day performance review.
